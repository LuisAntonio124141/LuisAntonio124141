Fluffy Pancakes

  • Time: 20 minutes
  • Serves: 4
Ingredients Quantity
All-purpose flour1 1/2 cups
Baking powder3 1/2 tsp
Salt1 tsp
White sugar1 tbsp
Milk1 1/4 cups
Egg1
Butter, melted3 tbsp

Step 1: In a large bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.

Step 2: Make a well in the center and pour in the milk, egg, and melted butter; mix until smooth.

Step 3: Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.

Step 4: Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1/4 cup for each pancake.

Step 5: Brown on both sides and serve hot.

Classic Guacamole

  • Time: 10 minutes
  • Serves: 4
Ingredients Quantity
Ripe avocados2
Onion, finely chopped1/4 cup
Fresh cilantro, chopped2 tbsp
Fresh lime juice1 tbsp
Saltto taste
Tomato, chopped (optional)1/2

Step 1: In a medium bowl, mash together the avocados, lime juice, and salt.

Step 2: Mix in onion, cilantro, and tomato (if using).

Step 3: Refrigerate 1 hour for best flavor, or serve immediately.

Chocolate Chip Cookies

  • Time: 30 minutes
  • Serves: 24 cookies
Ingredients Quantity
All-purpose flour2 1/4 cups
Baking soda1 tsp
Salt1 tsp
Butter, softened1 cup
White sugar3/4 cup
Brown sugar3/4 cup
Vanilla extract1 tsp
Eggs2
Chocolate chips2 cups

Step 1: Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).

Step 2: Combine flour, baking soda, and salt in a small bowl.

Step 3: In a large bowl, beat butter, white sugar, brown sugar, and vanilla extract until creamy.

Step 4: Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.

Step 5: Gradually mix in flour mixture. Stir in chocolate chips.

Step 6: Drop by rounded tablespoon onto ungreased baking sheets.

Step 7: Bake 9 to 11 minutes or until golden brown.

Source: Allrecipes - Best Chocolate Chip Cookies
